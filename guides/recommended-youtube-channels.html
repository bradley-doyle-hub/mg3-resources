Recommended YouTube Channels

Channels worth your time for extra practice outside class. After Effects first, then Blender. Each list starts with the best place to begin.

💡
Not sure where to start? After Effects: SonduckFilm. Blender: the Donut series from Blender Guru. Branch out once those click.

After Effects

Start at the top. Work down as you need more.

SonduckFilmStart here. Over 1,300 videos, beginner-friendly, and many come with project files you can download.
Ben MarriottClear tutorials for beginner to intermediate, plus career and workflow tips.
After Effects BasicsShort weekly lessons on keyframing, masking, and motion fundamentals.
Jake In MotionMotion design workflows, expressions, and software updates.
After Effects Tutorials w/ MikeyPractical lessons, beginner through advanced.
Adobe Creative Cloud (official)Feature updates and showcases. Not a tutorial channel, but good to know about.

Blender

Ordered from your first project to going deeper.

Blender Guru (Andrew Price)Start here. The Donut series takes you through modelling, texturing, lighting, and rendering in one project.
Grant AbbittSlower pace than Blender Guru. A good next stop if the Donut series felt fast.
PolyfjordAnimation and motion design, with high production value. Best once you're past the basics.
Ryan King ArtProcedural materials and shaders, for going deeper on texturing.
Blender SecretsOne-to-two-minute videos on single tools. Good for quick "how do I do X" questions.
Blender (official channel)Release notes and new features. Not a tutorial channel, but good to know about.
✓
That's the list. Pick one channel. Follow one project to the end. Then branch out.
