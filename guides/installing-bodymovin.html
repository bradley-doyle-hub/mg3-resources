Installing Bodymovin

Bodymovin is the After Effects extension that exports your animation as a Lottie file. Here's why that format matters, the walkthrough video, and the full written steps if you'd rather read than watch.

Why Lottie

This week's assignment moves off GIF and onto a lightweight, code-friendly format built entirely from vector shapes.

Smaller file size, by a wide margin.
Scales to any resolution without losing quality.
Plays back with real transparency.
Can be styled or controlled with code.

Why it matters: the same 5-second loop can be roughly 40KB as Lottie, 300KB as WebM, or 9MB as a GIF. On a real page, that's the difference between invisible and something nobody waits for.

FormatTypical size (5s loop)TransparencyBest for
Lottie (JSON)10–150KBYesUI motion, icons, illustrated animation
WebM / MP4150KB–1.5MBWebM: yes · MP4: noFilmed or rendered content, social platforms
Animated WebP200KB–2MBYesDirect GIF replacement
GIF2–15MBBinary onlyEmail, or platforms that accept nothing else
💡
The quick rule: illustrated or UI motion → Lottie. Filmed or rendered content → WebM, with an MP4 fallback. About to reach for a GIF? Use animated WebP instead, unless it's going in an email.

Watch the walkthrough

One-time setup. Once Bodymovin's installed, you're good for the rest of the semester.

Full written guide, with screenshots (13 steps)
  1. 1
    Navigate to AEscripts

    Go to aescripts.com in your web browser.

  2. 2
    Log in or sign up

    Click the profile icon in the top right menu to log in or create a new account.

  3. 3
    Search for Bodymovin

    Type "Bodymovin" into the search bar and select Bodymovin - Lottie from the dropdown results.

  4. 4
    "Purchase" the plugin

    Enter 0 (or your preferred amount) in the "Name Your Own Price" field, click Add to Cart, and complete checkout.

  5. 5
    Access your downloads

    Click the profile icon again and select Downloads & Licenses.

  6. 6
    Download the plugin zip

    Find Bodymovin in your product list and click Download to save the zip file to your machine.

  7. 7
    Navigate to the Support menu

    Scroll back to the top of the AEscripts page and click Support in the main navigation bar.

  8. 8
    Select ZXP Installer

    In the Support dropdown, under the "Tools" column, click ZXP Installer.

  9. 9
    Download the installer

    On the ZXP Installer page, click Download macOS or Download Windows depending on your system.

  10. 10
    Install the ZXP Installer

    Open the downloaded setup file and complete the install, e.g. dragging the app into your Applications folder on Mac.

  11. 11
    Extract the Bodymovin files

    Locate the Bodymovin zip you downloaded in step 6 and extract it. Locate the bodymovin.zxp file inside the new folder.

  12. 12
    Install Bodymovin

    Open the ZXP Installer app. Drag the bodymovin.zxp file into the window, click Install on the prompt, and wait for the success message.

  13. 13
    Launch in After Effects

    Fully quit and reopen After Effects if it was already running. Go to Window, then Extensions, then Bodymovin to open the plugin panel.

Extensions won't show up until you fully quit and relaunch After Effects, not just close the project.
✓
That's the install. One-time setup, you're good for the rest of the semester.
